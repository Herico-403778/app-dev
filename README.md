# A certain Scientific Railgun

---

*A Certain Scientific Railgun is a science‑fantasy story about power, responsibility, and the hidden dangers of a city built on experimentation. It follows Mikoto Misaka’s growth as she confronts injustices created by the very system that elevated her.*

**Lead Characters**
1. Mikoto Misaka - `Level 5 Electromaster and the titular “Railgun”; the central protagonist`

**Supporting Characters**
1. Kuroko Shirai `Level 4 Teleporter and Judgment officer; Mikoto’s partner and roommate`
2. Kazari Uiharu `Judgment member known for her calm personality and support skills`
3. Ruiko Saten `A Level 0 student and close friend of Uiharu; central to several character‑driven arcs`
4. Touma Kamijou `Cross‑series character who intervenes in key Railgun events`

### Official Season
